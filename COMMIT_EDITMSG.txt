refactor: Use Lombok for DTOs and configure MapStruct

This commit refactors all DTOs to use Lombok annotations for conciseness and readability. It also includes the necessary configurations and adjustments for MapStruct to work seamlessly with Lombok-generated code.

- **DTO Refactoring:**
  - Applied `@Data`, `@NoArgsConstructor`, and `@AllArgsConstructor` to all DTOs (`TransactionDTO`, `BudgetDTO`, `CategoryDTO`, `CategorySpendingDTO`, `ChangePasswordDTO`, `LoginDTO`, `LoginResponseDTO`, `MonthlyAnnualReportDTO`, `RegisterDTO`, `SummaryReportDTO`, `TransactionTypeDTO`, `UserDTO`).
  - Removed manual getters, setters, and constructors from DTOs.

- **Entity Refactoring:**
  - Applied `@Data`, `@NoArgsConstructor`, and `@AllArgsConstructor` to `TransactionType` entity to ensure proper setter generation for MapStruct.
  - Corrected `User` entity to use `List<Transaction>` instead of `List<Lancamento>`.

- **MapStruct Configuration and Adjustments:**
  - Enabled `mapstruct-processor` in `pom.xml` for annotation processing.
  - Adjusted `BudgetMapper` to use fully qualified name for `YearMonth` in expressions.
  - Corrected `TransactionMapper` to explicitly map `type.name` to `type` using a `@Named` method and added `ignore = true` for `user` and `category` in `toEntity` mapping.
  - Adjusted `TransactionTypeMapper` to explicitly map `id` and `name` for `toEntity` method.

- **Documentation:**
  - Created `ARCHITECTURE.md` to document the project's architecture, including the rationale behind DTOs, mappers (MapStruct), and reporting DTOs.
